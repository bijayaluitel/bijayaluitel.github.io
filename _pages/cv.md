During College 
-------


* LEAPS Project (Year 2 Summer, June 2021)
  * Associated with my project on the chemical inventory of a stellar protostar
  * Duties included: Using CASSIS to identify line emission from different species, MCMC fitting to the composite high resolution ALMA spectra of the protostar Serpens SMM1-b, imaging outflows associated with different species (particularly the outflow tracers) and using statcont to better fit the continuum to potentially improve the S/N of detections 
  * Supervisor: Dr. Niels Ligterink and Dr. Aida Ahmadi

* Undergraduate Research Assistant (Year 1 Semester 2 to Year 3 Semester 1; Year 1 Summer and Year 3 Summer)
  * Associated with my project on molecular gas and star formation in Cosmic Noon galaxies
  * Duties included: Reducing, calibrating and imaging VLA/ALMA data. Took part in journal club discussions (when held) and helped newer members of the research group get more comfortable with scripting and pipelines.  
  * Supervisor: Dr. Chelsea Sharon
