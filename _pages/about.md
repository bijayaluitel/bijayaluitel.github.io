Hello! I'm Bijaya Luitel, originally from Bhakapur, Nepal. When I was in high school, I was Nepal's first honoree at the International Olympiad in Astronomy and Astrophysics (IOAA) in 2016 and 2017, receiving Honorable Mentions (HM) both times. 

I graduated from Yale-NUS College (RIP) in 2023 with a major in the Physical Sciences.  I'm broadly interested in using the tools of observational astronomy to uncover new things about the universe. I'm currently looking for postgraduate opportunities.
