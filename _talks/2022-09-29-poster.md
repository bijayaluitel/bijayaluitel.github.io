A poster presentation I gave based on my BSc thesis topic. Most of the participants in the conference had little background in astronomy—the conference was mostly pitched to the Quantum/Materials Science researchers who dominate physics research in Singapore. Therefore, whilst my poster contained some preliminary results of SFR tracers and a CO(1-0) image, during the presentation phase and 3-minute poster pitch, I focused more on presenting the basic ideas that underly the study of molecular gas at high-z rather than presenting specific results from my thesis. <b>I won one of the three best posters on offer. </b>
